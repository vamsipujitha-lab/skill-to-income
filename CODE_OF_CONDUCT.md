# Code of conduct

This project is for students and learners, many of them beginners. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests
and discussions agrees to:

- **Be kind and patient.** Explain, don't mock. Every expert was a beginner once.
- **Be respectful.** No insults, harassment, or comments about anyone's background, religion, caste, gender, language,
  region or ability.
- **Keep it useful.** Stay on the topic of the roadmaps. No spam, self-promotion, paid-course adverts or exam dumps.
- **Be honest.** Only add videos, questions and facts you have checked yourself. Credit your sources.

## If something goes wrong
Report it to the maintainer privately (see `SECURITY.md` for how to reach them, or use GitHub's "Report content").
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.
Reports are kept private.
