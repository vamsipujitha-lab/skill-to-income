How to use this roadmap

This roadmap is our guide to the basics, based on our experience and research. It is not an official syllabus. Every video and tutorial we link is free; the videos belong to their creators, and we are not linked to any channel, website or institute. Adjust the plan to your own pace and goals. Once you are comfortable, you may choose a paid course from any website or institute you trust, but it is never required.
